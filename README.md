# Sleep Is Studying Too™ — Ship 11 of A Mirror of My Becoming™

> Encode by day, consolidate by night™ — the Neuroplasticize™ method. Sleep is studying too™.

## The story

This manual exists because someone I know was trying to study for the AWS Cloud Practitioner exam the way everyone is told to: nights, highlighters, videos at double speed, and a brain that kept nothing. The discipline wasn't broken. The match between the method and the brain holding it was — and no amount of extra hours fixes a mismatch; it just spends more hours proving the point.

So I built the thing I would hand a tired person. One loop, forty-five minutes a day, a free AI study partner, and a real night of sleep treated as the studying instead of the reward for studying. Test first, before any teaching. Let the misses build the plan. Anchor what matters in images your brain actually keeps. Walk the palace right before bed. Sleep eight hours — that's when the day gets saved. It worked for the student it was written for. Then I did what the fleet does with everything it carries: I took out the personal parts, kept the method, and shipped it as a template edition — written for one fellow student, shipped for everyone.

The method has a name: **Neuroplasticize™** — encode by day, consolidate by night™. Spaced review, plain language, and real sleep. That is the whole trick, and under AGPL it is yours.

## The method — Neuroplasticize™

Encode by day, consolidate by night™. The manual's sections map one-to-one onto the loop: find your style, run the system on one page, build the palace, take the test, set up the AI partner, sprint for fourteen days. No premium tier. The AGPL is the permission; the marks say whose it is.

## Find your style first

The manual opens with the five ways a brain stores information — visual, auditory, read/write, kinesthetic, spatial (memory palace) — and a sixty-second test that locks in which one is yours. Blends are welcome. The AI adapts the whole system to your letter from the second session on.
